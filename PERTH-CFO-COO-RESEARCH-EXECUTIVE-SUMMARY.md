## CONFIDENCE SCORING METHODOLOGY

Each firm assessed on 5 weighted dimensions:

1. **Domain Ownership (0.2):** Active website, HTTP 200 status, secure domain, current content
2. **LinkedIn Verification (0.2):** Active company page, founder profile consistency, employee count, recent activity
3. **Business Listing Consistency (0.2):** ABN register, business directories, contact consistency
4. **Website Service Clarity (0.2):** CFO/COO service articulation, pricing transparency, service packages, testimonials
5. **Founder/Leader Verifiability (0.2):** Name consistency, professional background, LinkedIn, media mentions

**Confidence Calculation:** Sum of (dimension score × weight) = total 0.0-1.0
