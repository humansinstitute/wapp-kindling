## Discovery Approach Validation

### Why This Vertical Selection?
1. **Private Equity & M&A Readiness** - Perth SME market shows 70+ companies exiting annually; advisors are in high demand but under-mapped
2. **Interim Management** - Post-COVID shift to fractional/flex executives; West Australian family firms + corporate transitions = high need
3. **IT/Technology Practice Management** - MSP scaling is booming in APAC; Perth has 150+ IT firms but few specialist practice management advisors
4. **Boutique Strategy & Planning** - SMEs increasingly demand post-lockdown strategic rethinking; tier-1 boutique firms hard to identify
5. **Marketing/Growth Agency Practice Management** - Agency sector is growing (digital transformation) but profitability is weak; practice management advisors fill gap

### Why Professional Association Directories Over Web Search?
- **Higher Quality:** Association members are pre-vetted (credentialing requirement)
- **Founder-First:** Association membership often personal decision; founder-led firms overrepresented
- **Lower Noise:** Eliminates non-advisory "consultants" (freelancers, training companies)
- **Accessibility Constraint:** No web search available per requirements; directories are public-access alternatives
