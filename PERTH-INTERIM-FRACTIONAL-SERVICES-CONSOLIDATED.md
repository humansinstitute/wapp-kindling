## MARKET INSIGHTS

### Fractional CFO Market (Perth)
- **Market Maturity:** Strong, with 5+ dedicated Perth-based providers plus national firms
- **Price Range:** $1,500–$6,000/month
- **Service Model:** Mix of boutique local providers and national firms
- **Key Differentiators:** Local presence, chartered accountant credentials, bundled services (bookkeeping, payroll, secretarial)
- **Growth Trend:** Increasing adoption by SMBs and startups as alternative to full-time CFO hire

### Interim Executive Placement
- **Market Leaders:** Six Degrees Executive, Future Leadership, Omera Partners, Derwent Search
- **Deployment Speed:** 72-hour placement capability
- **Service Depth:** Full-service solutions including coaching, onboarding, payroll, benefits
- **Market Growth:** Noticeable shift from permanent-only to interim/contract flexibility

### Executive Coaching
- **Market Diversity:** Solo practitioners to larger consulting groups
- **Specializations:** Sector-specific (mining, energy, government), transformation-focused, behavioral science-backed
- **ROI Messaging:** Consistent $4–$8 ROI per dollar invested across providers
- **Adoption:** Growing among mid-market and large organizations

### Business Advisory
- **Market Focus:** Primarily SME and startup segment
- **Service Model:** Hourly consulting and long-term advisory relationships
- **Typical Offerings:** Tax, accounting, strategy, R&D grants, export incentives
- **Competition:** Mix of boutique Perth firms and national accounting networks

### Executive Recruitment Ecosystem
- **Market Composition:** International firms (Robert Half, CSG Talent) coexist with local specialists (Scotford Fennessy, PeopleStart)
- **Trend:** Major recruitment firms increasingly adding interim/contract capability alongside permanent placement
- **Market Expansion:** New entrants focusing specifically on interim leadership solutions
