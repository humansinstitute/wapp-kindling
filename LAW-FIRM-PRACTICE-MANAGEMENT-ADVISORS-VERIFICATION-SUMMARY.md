## CONCLUSION

**Target Goal:** 15-40 verified firms minimum 0.75 confidence  
**Actual Result:** 12 verified firms, all meeting 0.75+ confidence threshold

Perth's law firm practice management advisory market is **mature but boutique-heavy**. The market is dominated by law firms offering practice management services as part of broader commercial/succession planning offerings rather than dedicated consultants. This is consistent with Perth's size relative to other Australian markets.

The 3 tier-1 specialized consultants (Red Jacket, LPMC, Peerpoint) combined with 9 boutique law firms offering practice management advisory create a credible market segment capable of supporting outreach campaigns focused on practice management, succession planning, and business advisory services.

**Market Characterization:** Perth's legal practice management advisory ecosystem is characterized by:
- High concentration in West Perth (42% of firms)
- Strong succession planning specialization (professional partnership focus)
- Established boutique positioning (average firm age 25+ years)
- Professional association integration (ALPMA leadership)
- Geographic clustering suggesting mature professional network

This market profile supports targeted outreach to decision-makers across both dedicated consultants and law firm leaders with practice management focus.
