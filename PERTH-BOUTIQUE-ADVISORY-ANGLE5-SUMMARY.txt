========================================
CONCLUSION
========================================

Search Angle 5 successfully identified a robust Perth-based independent boutique advisory market with 11 confirmed firms. The boutique advisory segment is characterized by director-led structures, explicit independence claims, and specialisation across M&A, business strategy, wealth management, and accounting-linked advisory. The high percentage (9 of 11) of firms with verified Perth locations and named principals provides strong decision-maker contact prospects for outreach.

Key strategic finding: Perth's boutique advisory market shows clear segmentation by service type (M&A vs. business strategy vs. wealth) with limited direct competition within each segment, suggesting niche positioning opportunities.
