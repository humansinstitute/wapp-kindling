================================================================================
GAPS IN CURRENT MARKET
================================================================================

1. LIMITED CONSTRUCTION SUCCESSION PLANNING SPECIALISTS
   Issue: Most succession/exit planning services are from generalist accountants or lawyers,
          not construction-specialist business advisors
   Opportunity: Boutique advisor specializing in construction owner transitions, with
                construction industry knowledge (cash flow cycles, subcontractor management,
                project pipeline valuation factors)

2. NO DEDICATED CONSTRUCTION OWNER EXIT STRATEGY FIRMS
   Issue: General business exit strategy firms (Oasis Partners, CFO On-Call) lack construction
          industry expertise; construction project managers don't offer business exit advisory
   Opportunity: Construction-focused exit strategy specialist bridging gap between construction
                project management and business valuation/M&A advisory

3. WEAK DELEGATION & OWNER DISENGAGEMENT COACHING
   Issue: Most construction coaches focus on revenue growth/systems but not on "owner leaving
          the business" coaching and team leadership development for transition
   Opportunity: Specialized construction coach focused on replacing owner in key functions
                (sales, technical, management) - build and empower leadership team

4. LIMITED LEADERSHIP TEAM DEVELOPMENT FOR CONSTRUCTION
   Issue: Most business coaches are 1:1 owner coaches; limited team development for construction
          company leadership teams preparing for growth/transition
   Opportunity: Construction-specialist offering team development, succession readiness
                assessment, and leadership capability building

5. LACK OF INTEGRATED ADVISORY (ADVISORY + ACCOUNTING + LEGAL)
   Issue: Construction owners must coordinate between separate business advisor, accountant,
          and lawyer for exit planning; no integrated advisory model
   Opportunity: Multi-service advisory combining business strategy, accounting, and legal
                for construction business exit/succession planning
