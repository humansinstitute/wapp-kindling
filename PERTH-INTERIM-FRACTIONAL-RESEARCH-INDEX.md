## KEY MARKET FINDINGS

### Fractional CFO Market (Perth)
- **Market Status:** Mature with 5+ dedicated local providers
- **Price Range:** $1,500–$6,000/month
- **Market Leaders (Local):** Your Financial Controller, Match Point Accounting, 360 Fox
- **Key Differentiators:** Local presence, chartered accountant credentials, bundled services
- **Growth Driver:** SMBs and startups seeking CFO expertise without full-time hire cost

### Interim Executive Placement
- **Market Leaders:** Six Degrees Executive, Future Leadership, Omera Partners, Derwent Search
- **Deployment Speed:** 72-hour placement capability common
- **Service Depth:** Full-service including coaching, onboarding, payroll, benefits
- **Trend:** Shift from permanent-only to interim/contract flexibility

### Executive Coaching
- **Market Diversity:** Solo practitioners to larger consulting groups
- **Specializations:** Sector-specific (mining, energy, government), transformation, behavioral science
- **ROI Messaging:** Consistent $4–$8 per dollar invested across providers
- **Perth Strength:** Award-winning practitioners with Fortune 500 client bases (e.g., Margo Halbert)

### Business Advisory
- **Market Focus:** SME and startup segment
- **Service Model:** Hourly consulting and long-term advisory relationships
- **Average Rate:** ~$300 + GST per hour
- **Typical Services:** Tax, accounting, strategy, R&D grants, export incentives

### Executive Recruitment Ecosystem
- **Market Composition:** International firms coexist with local specialists
- **Growth Trend:** Major firms increasingly adding interim/contract capability
- **Key Players:** Robert Half (international), Scotford Fennessy (local specialist)
